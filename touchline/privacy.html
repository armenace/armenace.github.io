Touchline privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Touchline is a football management game made by Fennix. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. Touchline has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your careers, settings and any data packs you install are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Internet access

The game works offline. The app only connects to the internet when you choose to install the optional community data pack. It then downloads two files from a public web server. That request does not include any information about you. The server may keep standard technical logs, such as an IP address, as all web servers do.

Children

Touchline does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
